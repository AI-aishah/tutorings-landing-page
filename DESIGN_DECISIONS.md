# Tutorings Landing Page — Design Decisions

- **Landing-page type:** A dependency-free storytelling page for personalized English and IELTS tutoring.
- **Core concept:** The Learning Path.
- **Emotional message:** From confusion to confidence.
- **Core promise:** Your journey to mastering English starts with a clear path.
- **Main value:** Personalized one-to-one English and IELTS learning that develops practical communication and supports IELTS goals.
- **Main audience:** University students, IELTS candidates planning to study abroad, and young professionals seeking career growth.
- **Main emotional problem:** Learners feel lost and discouraged despite trying many resources.
- **Desired result:** Clarity, confidence, motivation, and practical English ability.
- **Messaging objective:** Help overwhelmed visitors see Tutorings as a supportive, direct path toward their English goals and feel ready to book a first lesson.
- **Tone:** Premium, calm, supportive, modern, professional, and human.
- **Visual direction:** Centered, spacious, SaaS-inspired, and educational without feeling childish.
- **Path system:** Segmented abstract paths move from scattered effort to guidance, progress, confidence, and destination; responsive versions simplify rather than squeeze.
- **Color and type:** Navy text and light surfaces establish calm clarity. Purple and blue gradients mark guidance and progress. Manrope is the primary typeface with system fallbacks.
- **Motion and accessibility:** Motion is restrained and progressive. Semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and complete reduced-motion states are required.
- **Claims policy:** Unverified testimonials and results remain visibly labeled placeholders. No names, scores, ratings, counts, percentages, or guarantee details are invented.
